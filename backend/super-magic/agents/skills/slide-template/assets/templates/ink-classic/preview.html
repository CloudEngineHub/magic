Two-Column Comparison · compare-block
Carbon Sink Status
When NEP > 0, forests are net absorbers of CO₂, acting as carbon sinks. Healthy forest ecosystems typically remain in carbon sink status, absorbing large amounts of atmospheric carbon annually.
Carbon Source Status
When NEP < 0, forests are net emitters of CO₂, acting as carbon sources. Fires, pests, overlogging and other disturbances can shift forests from sinks to sources.
