Core Design Principle
This handbook contains complete designs for 6 core interactive activities throughout the semester.
Experience Before Theory
Experience teamwork challenges under information asymmetry, and feel the value of clear communication
"What kind of instructions are effective? Why does 'allowing questions' change the outcome?"
Introduce after activity: Groupthink and Constructive Conflict concepts
Team Charter: Clear roles / Decision methods / Conflict resolution rules
Consensus requires full agreement — no forced votes
Process Step Components
1
Round 1 · 10 min
Follow rules: Architect gives directions, Worker wears blindfold, Worker may NOT ask questions
2
Reflect · 5 min
Observer feedback, whole group discusses problems and obstacles
3
Round 2 · 10 min
Workers MAY ask questions, try building again
