Persona Research

Why trial users churn in week two

Research question: Why do our trial users stop using the app in the second week?

User research · JTBD 7 of 8 interviews completed 1 focus group 12 web sources 2026-10-18

Respondents in this study are AI-simulated personas built from public sources and stated assumptions, not real users. Treat findings as hypotheses to validate with the attached questionnaire.

Executive summary

Week-two churn is driven less by missing features than by the absence of a first "win" in week one: users who never connect the product to a recurring task stop opening it once the novelty fades.[Q1][S1]

Recommendations

  1. P1Guide every trial user to one recurring task in the first three days

    Why: 5 of 7 personas described the product as "nice but not part of my routine".[Q2] Validate: ask real trial users which task they tried first.

Findings by segment

Owner-operators (3 personas)

Trust comes from peers, not features

Medium confidence

Owner-operators delay adoption until someone they know uses the product; feature lists did not move them in any interview.[S2]

Zhang Wei · "Not before I see a friend using it."

Counter-evidence: one efficiency-driven owner adopted after a free trial without peer proof.[Q4]

Disagreements and open questions

Value vs. trust: in the focus group, efficiency-driven owners accepted the price immediately, while relationship-driven owners rejected recording customers at any price.[Q5]

Assumptions and limitations

  • Respondents are AI-simulated personas, not real users. Their answers reflect the profiles and the model's general knowledge.
  • Effective sample: 7 completed interviews out of 8 planned; 1 interview failed (persona profile incomplete).
  • Social media evidence was collected through search engines only and does not represent full platform coverage.
  • Persona traits marked "(inferred)" in transcripts are model assumptions, not sourced facts.
