Observation

Why this place
makes people want to stay

After the rain, the garden has no obvious attraction. What draws people in are low details: wet brick joints, half-shaded seats, and a path that loops just enough.

These modest arrangements turn a walk from passing through into pausing for a while.

Note

The best public spaces are often not louder; they are easier to relax in.

A slow path can hold people better than a large lawn. Field note
